# HLD: The Painter's Journey

**Pitch:** A 2D action game where you fight through rooms, but every room you clear adds a new part to your painting.

**Genre / view / platform:** 2D side view action, PC

**Target experience:** The player feels tense but in control. Every fight is hard but fair, and seeing the painting come together makes them want to continue.

**Core loop:** Enter room → fight → clear → reveal part of the painting

**Win:** Beat the boss in the last room and see the finished painting.

**Lose:** Health reaches zero, and the player restarts the current room.

**Controls:**
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A / D | Move |
| Space | Jump |
| J | Brush attack |
| Esc | Pause |

**Must have features:** Main menu and ending, move/jump/attack, health and restart, two enemy types, 4 rooms, painting reveal after each room, boss room, sound and music.

**Look and sound:** Simple 2D art. The world starts dull and grey, and the painting is the most colorful thing in the game. Calm music between rooms and faster music in fights.

**Out of scope:** Brush powers, more than 4 rooms plus the boss, multiplayer, story dialogue.
